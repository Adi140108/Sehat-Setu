# Sehat Setu — Hackathon Judging Demo Script

## Key Presentation Message
> "India has world-class public healthcare schemes like PM-JAY and Jan Aushadhi Kendras, but millions of underserved citizens don't know where to go or what documents they need. **Sehat Setu does not try to replace doctors with AI—it helps people navigate the healthcare system that already exists.**"

---

## 3-Minute Demo Walkthrough

### 1. Multilingual Voice Facility Locator (1 Minute)
- **Click**: Top Banner **"Demo 1: Hindi Voice Search"**.
- **User Voice Query**: *"Mujhe paas mein sarkari hospital chahiye."*
- **Action**: System transcribes voice, detects Hindi, classifies `FIND_FACILITY` intent, and displays verified hospitals sorted by GPS distance with Leaflet map pins, phone call buttons, and Google Maps directions.

### 2. Red Emergency Safety Path (45 Seconds)
- **Click**: Top Banner **"Demo 2: Emergency (Red)"**.
- **User Voice Query**: *"Mere seene mein bahut dard ho raha hai."*
- **Action**: Safety filter detects high-risk chest pain pattern, halts normal navigation, and launches the **RED EMERGENCY ALERT** screen with an immediate **108 Call** dialer and nearest trauma center list. No diagnostic guesswork.

### 3. Deterministic Scheme Engine & Document Checklist (45 Seconds)
- **Click**: Top Banner **"Demo 3: Scheme & Docs"**.
- **Action**: Input age 70 for senior citizen scheme check. Evaluates Ayushman Vaya Vandana 70+ and PM-JAY rules deterministically. Generates Aadhaar & Ration Card document checklists with missing document guidance.

### 4. ASHA Worker Support Desk & WhatsApp Simulator (30 Seconds)
- **Switch Tab**: **ASHA / Volunteer Portal**. View incoming citizen assistance requests and mark status as *In Progress*.
- **Switch Tab**: **WhatsApp Demo Mode**. Send simulated WhatsApp text message to demonstrate zero-friction messaging integration.
